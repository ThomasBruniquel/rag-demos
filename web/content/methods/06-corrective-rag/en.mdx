name: Corrective and adaptive RAG (agent)

sourceLabel: "Yan et al., Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation"
need: A customer assistant that picks the right action, refuses to guess and knows when to hand over to a human.
how:
  - label: Route
    text: The agent sizes up the question (simple, complex, off-topic) and chooses between the FAQ, a tool (catalogue, orders) or asking for details.
  - label: Correct
    text: It grades what it found; if that isn't enough, it rephrases and searches again.
  - label: Escalate
    text: If doubt remains, it won't answer at random and opens a ticket for a human.
strengths:
  - Fewer made-up answers when information is missing.
  - It can act (track an order), not just quote the FAQ.
  - Handing over to a human is planned, logged and measurable.
limits:
  - Several model calls per question, so a higher cost.
  - More scenarios to test than a simple RAG.
choose:
  - Customer support, internal helpdesks, questions that lead to an action.
  - "Multiple sources: FAQ, product database, order system."
avoid:
  - "A plain static FAQ: classic RAG is enough."
  - A very low budget per conversation.
adaptation: "The demo builds the agent with LangGraph, applies Bedrock Guardrails and creates escalation tickets in a small back office visible on the site."

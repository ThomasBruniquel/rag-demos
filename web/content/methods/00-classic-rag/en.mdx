---
name: Classic RAG
origin: Method published by Facebook AI Research (May 2020)
sourceLabel: "Lewis et al., Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks"
need: Have a language model answer from your documents rather than from its own memory.
how:
  - label: Split
    text: Documents are cut into fixed-size chunks, turned into vectors and stored in a database.
  - label: Retrieve
    text: The question becomes a vector too; the closest chunks are fetched.
  - label: Answer
    text: The model writes its answer from those chunks and cites them.
strengths:
  - Simple to build and to explain.
  - Cheap to run, even at large volumes.
  - Every answer comes with sources you can check.
limits:
  - Fixed-size chunks break tables, sections and context.
  - Exact terms (references, clause numbers) are poorly matched by vectors alone.
  - Nothing checks that the answer actually says what the sources say.
choose:
  - A first document assistant that needs to ship fast.
  - Plain documents written in running text.
avoid:
  - Documents full of tables, diagrams or deep structure.
  - Questions that require combining many documents.
seo:
  title: Classic RAG, the baseline for every project
  description: "How a classic RAG pipeline works (chunking, vector search, generation), its strengths and limits, and why every project on this site is benchmarked against it."

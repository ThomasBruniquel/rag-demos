how:
  - label: Read the contents
    text: The report's table of contents is the way in, instead of fixed-size chunks.

strengths:
  - Tables and sections stay whole.
  - Citations point to a precise section that is easy to check.
  - The authors report the right section found first time in 82.6% of cases, against 59.5% for keyword search.
limits:
  - Documents need real structure (contents page, headings).
  - Several model calls per question, so slower than vector search.
choose:
  - Annual reports, standards, manuals, regulatory documentation.
  - Questions about one specific section or table.
avoid:
  - Short or unstructured text (emails, tickets).
  - Instant answers over millions of pages.

tagline: A document chatbot that shows its sources, ships in days, and reads your PDFs too.
problem: "A small company wants an assistant that answers from its own documentation, with sources, without spending months on it or paying for heavy infrastructure. Staff keep asking the same questions; the answers exist, but they are scattered across hundreds of pages."
corpus:
  name: "\"Entreprendre\" (business) help pages from Service-Public.fr, the French government portal (DILA)"
  size: "~3,000 pages, in French"
  licence: Etalab Open Licence 2.0
  url: https://www.data.gouv.fr/datasets/fiches-pratiques-et-ressources-entreprendre-service-public-fr/
examples:
  - How do I register as a sole trader (micro-entreprise)?
  - At what turnover do I have to charge VAT?
  - What support is there for hiring a first employee?
  - "With your PDF: what are the key dates in this document?"
architecture:
  alt: "Architecture: the browser calls a Lambda function, which queries Bedrock Knowledge Bases (S3 Vectors index) for the help pages, or reads the visitor's cached PDF directly, then generates the answer with Claude on Bedrock."
  choices:
    - title: A managed RAG service (Bedrock Knowledge Bases)
      why: Chunking, indexing and citations come built in, leaving very little code to maintain, which is what a small company needs.
    - title: The visitor's whole PDF in context, cached
      why: "A 20-page document fits in the model's context: no risk of missing the right passage, and prompt caching avoids paying to read it again for every question."
    - title: S3 Vectors as the index
      why: No fixed cost, a few cents of storage per month.
  discarded:
    - title: OpenSearch Serverless
      why: Several hundred euros a month at minimum, impossible to justify for 3,000 pages.
    - title: A custom retrieval pipeline
      why: More control, but weeks of work for little quality gain on simple pages.
pipeline:
  alt: "Pipeline: DILA archive downloaded, XML converted to Markdown by a Lambda function, stored on S3, Knowledge Base synchronised. For uploads: stored under a session prefix, deleted automatically after one hour."
  facts:
    - label: Volume
      value: "~3,000 pages"
    - label: Ingestion time
      value: "~20 min"
    - label: Ingestion cost
      value: "< €2"
    - label: Updates
      value: Monthly (new DILA archive)
    - label: Uploaded documents
      value: Deleted after one hour
evaluation:

The architecture deliberately avoids sophistication: a managed service for the knowledge base, and the visitor's document read in full. That is what makes it possible to ship a reliable assistant to a small company within days, then run it for a few euros a month.
